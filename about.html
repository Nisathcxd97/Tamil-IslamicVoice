About Us

Who We Are

Tamil Islamic Voice is an online Islamic radio station dedicated to spreading Quran, Hadith, Islamic lectures, Islamic reminders and other beneficial programs to listeners worldwide — in the beautiful Tamil language.

Broadcasting 24 hours a day, 7 days a week from Thampalagamam, Trincomalee, Sri Lanka, our voice reaches Tamil-speaking Muslims across the globe — from Colombo to Chennai, Dubai to Toronto — offering authentic Islamic knowledge, peace and guidance anywhere, anytime.

"The best of you are those who learn the Qur'an and teach it."
— Sahih al-Bukhari

Our Mission

To make authentic Islamic knowledge accessible to every Tamil-speaking Muslim, free of charge, through radio, video and digital media — nurturing faith, character and community.

Our Vision

A world where every listener can connect with the Quran and the Sunnah — a trusted Islamic voice that inspires hearts, enlightens minds and unites the Ummah.

Our Values

  • Authenticity — knowledge from authentic sources
  • Accessibility — free for everyone, everywhere
  • Respect — a warm, peaceful voice for all
  • Community — serving the Ummah with love
