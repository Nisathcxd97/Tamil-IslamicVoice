About Us

Sharing Islamic Knowledge Worldwide

Tamil Islamic Voice is an online Islamic radio dedicated to spreading authentic Islamic knowledge to the Tamil-speaking community, wherever they are.

Tamil Islamic Voice is a dedicated online Islamic radio platform broadcasting Quran recitation, Hadith, Islamic lectures, reminders and other beneficial programs to listeners worldwide — in the beautiful Tamil language.

From Thampalagamam, Trincomalee, Sri Lanka, we reach homes, hearts and minds across the globe, offering a peaceful voice of faith, guidance and hope, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
