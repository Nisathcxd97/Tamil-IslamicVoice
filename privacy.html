Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Tamil Islamic Voice ("we", "our", "us") operates this website and online radio service. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we handle and how.

Contact: tamilislamicvoice@gmail.com · WhatsApp +94 75 737 4750 · Siraj Nagar, Thampalagamam, Trincomalee, Sri Lanka.

2. Information We Collect

We are a listening platform — not a data company. We collect only what you voluntarily provide:

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message, used solely to reply to you. Messages are delivered via your own WhatsApp or email app — nothing is stored on our servers.
  • Preferences: your chosen theme (light/dark) and volume setting are saved in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.
  • Anonymous analytics (if enabled by the hosting provider): aggregated visitor counts only.

We do not collect personal data through the radio stream itself, and we do not sell or rent any personal information.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage purely for convenience features: your chosen colour theme, volume level and mute preference. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Our site integrates the following external services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Streaming: Zeno.fm and other radio stream providers power our live audio.
  • YouTube: embedded videos from our channel "Tamil Muslim TV".
  • Google Maps: an embedded map showing our location in Trincomalee.
  • Google Fonts / CDN libraries: Bootstrap, Font Awesome, AOS.

These services may set their own cookies when you interact with them. We encourage you to review their policies.

5. Offline / PWA Support

As a Progressive Web App, a service worker caches parts of this website on your device so pages load faster and remain available offline. The cache contains only site assets (styles, scripts, images) — never your personal information.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have sent us at any time by emailing tamilislamicvoice@gmail.com. We will respond promptly and honour valid requests.

7. Children's Privacy

Our content is family-friendly and safe for all ages. We never knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The latest version will always be available on this page with a revised date.
