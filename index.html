📖 About Us

Tamil Islamic Voice is a dedicated radio platform bringing authentic Islamic content to the Tamil-speaking community worldwide. Our mission is to educate, inspire, and connect through the timeless message of Islam.

Based in Siraj Nagar, Thampalagamam, Trincomalee, Sri Lanka, we serve listeners across the globe with a variety of programs including Qur'an recitation, lectures, nasheeds, and community discussions.
