About Tamil Islamic Voice

Tamil Islamic Voice is a community-driven radio channel dedicated to spreading the message of peace, unity, and Islamic values. Based in Trincomalee, Sri Lanka, we serve Tamil-speaking Muslims around the world with authentic content.

Our programming includes Quranic recitations, Islamic lectures, daily supplications, and community updates — all delivered with clarity and devotion.
